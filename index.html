1. About this policy

This policy explains how the rahi mobile app ("rahi", "the app") handles information. rahi is published by Mirab, a business registered in India ("we", "us"). It applies to the rahi app distributed through Google Play.

By using rahi you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. rahi has no user accounts, no login, no contact forms and no servers that receive data from the app. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, messages, device identifiers or advertising ID.

Everything the app needs to work, including the database of places, runs on your phone.

3. Location

rahi's core feature is showing places worth visiting near you, so it asks for permission to use your device's location.

  • What it is used for: calculating the distance from you to nearby places and sorting them, and as the starting point when you plan a trip.
  • Where it goes: nowhere. Your location is processed only on your phone. It is not uploaded, shared, sold or stored on any server.
  • When it is used: only while you are using the app. rahi does not track your location in the background.
  • Your choice: you can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your phone's settings. You can still use rahi by choosing any place in India as your location inside the app.

When you choose a location manually, the search is done on your phone against the app's built-in list of places. Your search is not sent anywhere.

4. Data stored on your device

The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone:

DataPurpose
Bookmarked placesSo you can find places you liked later
Saved tripsSo you can open, edit and share your trip plans
Settings (language, distance range, chosen location)So the app remembers your preferences
Downloaded photos and AI model (optional)So the app works without internet

We cannot see or access this data. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

5. How rahi uses the internet

rahi is built to work offline. It uses the internet only for:

  • Place photos: photos are loaded from our image storage (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region). You can download all photos once from Settings to use them offline.
  • AI model download (optional): the trip planner needs an AI model of about 2.6 GB. It is downloaded once from Hugging Face, only when you choose to download it, and then runs entirely on your phone.

When your phone downloads a photo or the model, the hosting service receives the technical information that every internet request carries, such as your IP address and the file requested. This is handled by the hosting provider under its own policy (see section 7). No location, bookmarks, trips or other personal data are sent with these requests.

6. Permissions

PermissionWhy rahi needs it
Location (approximate and precise)To show places near you, sorted by distance. Used only while the app is open.
InternetTo load place photos and to download the optional AI model.
Network stateTo check whether you are online before a download, so the app can work offline otherwise.

rahi does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, calendar, photos or files.

7. Third-party services

rahi does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. Some features hand off to, or download from, other services:

  • Google Maps: tapping a directions or route button opens the Google Maps app or website with the destination. From that point Google's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Amazon Web Services: hosts place photos. AWS Privacy Notice.
  • Hugging Face: hosts the AI model file. Hugging Face Privacy Policy. The model is Google's Gemma, used under the Gemma Terms of Use.
  • Wikipedia: some descriptions link to their Wikipedia source. Opening a link takes you to Wikipedia, under the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.
  • Android text-to-speech: the read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone.
  • Sharing: when you share or export a trip, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or email). rahi shares only what you choose to share.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these services. Please read their policies.

8. AI trip planner

  • The trip planner runs on your phone. Your trip details are never sent to us or to any AI service online.
  • Plans are generated automatically and may contain mistakes, such as wrong timings, distances or opening hours. Please check important details before you travel.
  • The planner only suggests places from rahi's own database of places worth visiting.

9. Content standards

rahi is a travel guide. It contains information about public places only: landmarks, places of worship, parks, lakes, hills and beaches.

  • rahi has no sexual, vulgar or violent content.
  • rahi has no betting, gambling, lottery or real-money games.
  • rahi has no in-app purchases and no advertising.
  • Place information comes from public sources, including Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), and our own editing. It is provided as is and may be incomplete or out of date.

10. No medical, legal or safety advice

rahi does not provide medical, health, legal, financial or safety advice. Travel information is for general guidance only. Check local conditions, weather, opening hours, entry rules and travel advisories before visiting any place, and follow the guidance of local authorities.

11. No user-to-user interaction

rahi has no chat, comments, reviews, profiles or any way for users to contact or interact with each other. Users cannot post or upload content in the app.

12. Children

rahi is a general travel app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox. Downloads use secure (HTTPS) connections. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is no account or server database that could be breached.

14. Your choices and rights

  • Location: allow or deny it in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Downloads: photos and the AI model are optional. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings.
  • Your data: since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You control everything on your phone.

We respect your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws. If you have any request or question, contact us (section 18).

15. Data retention and deletion

We retain no personal data. Data stored on your phone by rahi stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or using "Clear storage" in Android settings permanently removes all of it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.

17. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
