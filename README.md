# rahi

**Places worth visiting, near you.** In English and six Indian languages, even without internet.

rahi is a free Android app that shows you the temples, mosques, churches, gurudwaras, parks, lakes, hills, beaches and landmarks around you the moment you open it. You don't need to search, sign up or type anything.

## Why rahi

Most travel apps assume English, a fast connection and an account. They focus on famous sights and bookings. rahi is built for the rest of India: the neighbourhood temple, the town lake, the hill viewpoint two hours away, in the language you read and where the signal drops.

## What it does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Nothing to type** | Open it and nearby places are listed, nearest first. |
| **Works offline** | Place data is inside the app. Download photos and the AI model once, and you need no internet after that. |
| **Seven languages** | English, हिन्दी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം and বাংলা. Names, addresses and descriptions all switch. |
| **All of India** | 60K+ places across 36 states and union territories. |
| **Listen to a place** | Descriptions read aloud in your language. |
| **AI trip planner** | Day-by-day plans written by AI that runs on your phone. Save them, export as PDF, share on WhatsApp. |
| **Your distance** | Look within 0–50, 50–100 or 100–150 km. |
| **Be anywhere in India** | Set any city as your location and explore before you travel. |
| **Private** | No account. Your location, bookmarks and trips never leave your phone. |
| **Free, really free** | No ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier. |

Every location has been checked and corrected where needed. Descriptions are still being added; places without one say "Description coming soon".

## Your privacy

- rahi collects **no personal data** and has **no servers** of its own.
- Location is used only on your phone, to find nearby places. It is never sent anywhere.
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no login, no chat between users.
- The internet is used only to load place photos and, if you choose, to download the trip-planner AI model.

Full privacy policy: **[mithilesh-lala.github.io/rahi-privacy](https://mithilesh-lala.github.io/rahi-privacy/)**

## Requirements

- Android 7.0 or newer.
- Trip planner: Android 11 or newer, about 4 GB RAM and 3.5 GB free space for the AI model (optional).
